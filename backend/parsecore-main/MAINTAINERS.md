---

### Responsibilities

Maintainers agree to:

- 🔍 Review and respond to pull requests within 5 business days
- 🏷️ Triage and respond to new issues within 3 business days
- 🎯 Keep ParseCore **bit-exact** with the official osu! implementation, including porting future pp/star rating reworks promptly
- 🧭 Guide the technical direction of the project
- 🛡️ Enforce the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- ✅ Ensure contributions meet the standards in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)

---

### Becoming a Maintainer

Contributors who consistently demonstrate:

- High-quality code contributions (especially parity-preserving algorithm work)
- Thoughtful code reviews
- Active participation in issue discussions and the [Discord community](https://discord.gg/9p7whE7QxQ)

may be invited to become maintainers. Maintainership is granted by consensus of the existing core maintainers.
